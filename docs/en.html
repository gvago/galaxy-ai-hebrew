Hebrew for Galaxy AI

Samsung's Galaxy AI already supports Hebrew in Writing Assist, Photo Assist and Creative Studio. It does not support Hebrew in Live Translate, Interpreter, Note Assist or Transcript Assist: the features people use to talk, listen and take notes. We are Hebrew-speaking Galaxy owners asking Samsung to add it.

Sign the survey (3 minutes)

Campaign reference: HE-GALAXY-AI-2026 · Independent volunteer campaign. We are not Samsung and do not represent Samsung.

The ask

Add Hebrew to the next Galaxy AI language expansion on otherwise eligible devices. If rollout must be phased, open an opt-in Hebrew beta with at least one communication feature (Live Translate or Interpreter) and one productivity feature (Transcript Assist or Note Assist). Within 30 days: a named liaison, the current roadmap status, and the date of the next update.

In return we offer: a documented cohort of Hebrew-speaking Galaxy owners, opt-in beta testers across devices and use cases, introductions to Hebrew speech, language, translation and accessibility experts, and a rights-aware evaluation plan for real conversational Hebrew.

How to join: two steps

  1. Sign the survey. 3 minutes. Only aggregate numbers are published. Open the survey.
  2. Send Samsung your own request. In your own words, once, and keep the case number. Guide below.

Samsung has said features like Live Translate run three separate components (speech recognition, machine translation, text-to-speech), each trained on region-specific data gathered with local institutions. That is real work. Hebrew has an open ecosystem that can shorten it: speech models and data from ivrit.ai, text models from DICTA, and commercial Hebrew speech services already on the market. That shows feasibility; it is not a Samsung implementation.

FAQ

"Galaxy AI has no Hebrew", right?

Wrong, and we do not say it. Hebrew exists in Writing Assist, Photo Assist and Creative Studio. The gap is in the communication and productivity features.

Can Samsung "just switch Hebrew on"?

No. It needs engineering, data-rights review, integration and validation. We are asking for a roadmap and a beta, not magic.

Why not send everyone the same text to Samsung?

Identical texts look like spam and count as one request. A personal request, in your words, with a case number, is worth far more.

What happens to my data?

See the privacy notice. In short: email for de-duplication, aggregate numbers only in public, separate consent for everything else, deletion on a fixed date.

Who is behind this?

Guy Vago, a Galaxy S24 Ultra owner in Israel, currently alone. Code, sources and the decision log are open at github.com/gvago/galaxy-ai-hebrew. Want to help? That is the place.

Guide: how to send Samsung your own request

Do not copy a template. One shared subject line, six questions, answers in your own words. One submission per person. Follow up on the same case, never open a new one.

Before you start

  • Find your model and One UI version: Settings → About phone → Software information.
  • You will write 5-8 sentences. Your words matter more than polish.
  • Write down the case number you get. You need it for the survey and for follow-up.

Subject line (exactly this, with your model)

HE-GALAXY-AI-2026 - Hebrew for core Galaxy AI - [your device model]

Answer the six questions

  1. Which Galaxy device and One UI version do you use?
  2. Which unavailable Hebrew feature matters most to you: Live Translate, Interpreter, Note Assist or Transcript Assist?
  3. Describe one real situation where it would have helped you.
  4. What do you do instead today?
  5. Would you join an official opt-in Hebrew beta if Samsung opened one?
  6. Ask: "Please record this as a product feature request and send me a reference number."

Where to send it

  1. Samsung Members app (preferred, reaches the engineering queue): Support tab → Feedback / Ask the experts / Suggestion (being verified on an Israeli device). Not "Error report", that is for malfunctions. Your submission and the answer appear under "Check the feedback you sent".
  2. Samsung Israel email form: samsung.com/il/support/contact → "Send email". Samsung says it replies within one business day.
  3. Chat or WhatsApp (same page): ask the agent to log a feature request and give you a case number.

Do not use the CEO-office form as an individual. The campaign uses it once, at the escalation step, referencing everyone's cases.

After you send

Add the case number to the survey. A reply like "forwarded to the relevant team" is expected: reply once on the same case and ask for a status date.

No hostility, no tagging employees, no review-bombing, no asking Samsung to drop another language. We are asking for Hebrew to be added, factually and politely.

Privacy notice

Who we are. "Galaxy AI בעברית" (reference HE-GALAXY-AI-2026) is a volunteer, non-commercial campaign run by Guy Vago, guy@vago.co.il. We are not Samsung and do not represent Samsung or any partner organization.

Why we collect data. To document how many Hebrew-speaking Galaxy owners want Hebrew in these features; to remove duplicates; to contact people who separately asked to hear about an official beta; and to publish aggregate statistics only.

What we collect. Only what the survey asks: email; age confirmation (18+); device model and One UI version; country/region; languages; which features you would use; a short story in your words; your workaround; whether it affected a purchase; an optional accessibility note; and your separate choices for beta contact and for quoting. We never ask for: IMEI, serial number, receipt, ID document, Samsung password, address, or screenshots.

What we publish. Counts and percentages only. Groups smaller than 10 are not broken out. Your story is quoted only if you ticked the box, exactly as you chose (anonymous, or first name and city).

Who can see your data. Identifiable data is visible to the campaign lead and one named backup, once appointed. Partners, journalists and Samsung receive aggregates and consented quotes only. We do not sell, rent or share raw data.

Where it is stored. Google Forms and Google Sheets in the campaign's Google account with two-factor authentication. Exports on encrypted disks only.

How long we keep it. Identifiable data is deleted by 31 March 2027 or 30 days after the final report, whichever is earlier, unless you opted in to beta contact, in which case we keep your email until the beta question is resolved or you withdraw. Aggregates are kept.

Beta consent is limited. We pass your email to Samsung only if an official opt-in beta opens and only under written terms. It is not permission to train models on anything you provide. We will not send audio or personal data without such terms.

Your rights. At any time, email guy@vago.co.il to see, correct, withdraw consent, or delete everything. We act within 14 days. Withdrawal does not affect aggregates already published.
