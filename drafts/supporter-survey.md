# Supporter survey: draft v0.1 (not published)

Target tool: Google Forms (Lean tier, free, exports to Sheets). One form, two language versions or a bilingual form with EN under HE. Every required field is marked `*`. Everything else is optional. Adults only.

Reporting categories this form must be able to produce (plan §9):
- **Confirmed supporter** = unique confirmed email (Q1)
- **Self-reported Galaxy owner** = Q3 ≠ "I don't currently own a Galaxy device"
- **Documented Samsung submission** = tracked separately in the case tracker, not in this form
- **Beta candidate** = Q14 checked

Never collected: IMEI, serial number, receipt, ID document, Samsung account password, home address, unredacted screenshots.
