# Hebrew for Galaxy AI: factual dossier and request

Ref. HE-GALAXY-AI-2026 · v1.0 · 15 September 2026 · Submitted by Guy Vago, guy@vago.co.il · Public: gvago.github.io/galaxy-ai-hebrew

## 1. The gap, per Samsung

Samsung Israel's official Galaxy AI page (as of July 2026) lists Hebrew among the 41 languages for Writing Assist, Photo Assist and Creative Studio, and not among the 22 languages for Call Assist (Live Translate), Interpreter, Note Assist and Transcript Assist. Verified on device: an Israeli Galaxy S24 Ultra (One UI 8.5, 9 Sep 2026) offers 29 language packs, none Hebrew; the Live Translate language picker is identical.

## 2. What Samsung Israel has already said

Answering a personal request through the email form, Samsung Israel customer service wrote (9 Sep 2026) that development teams are working to expand language support "and in particular to add full Hebrew support", with no launch estimate. The next day (10 Sep 2026) it added that suggestions are considered by the global development team "according to demand and available resources", and that demand is measured through reports in the Members app.

We accept that frame. This dossier is the demand, documented.

## 3. Demand to date

10 survey respondents (8 to 15 Sep 2026), all in Israel. 8 on Galaxy S, 1 on Z Fold, 1 on A series; models from S23 to S26 Ultra and Fold8. 7 of 10 would use these features daily, 3 a few times a week. Wanted pair: Hebrew-English (8 of 10). Reported use cases, in their words: training employees, phone calls with English-speaking staff, communicating with colleagues abroad, a Spanish-speaking partner, travel, reading Hebrew notifications aloud. Current workarounds: Google Translate, Google TTS, third-party apps, "I have no other way". 2 personal requests to Samsung documented (email + Members). The numbers are small and reported as they are; the metric we keep measuring is Members reports.

## 4. Why it is feasible

Samsung explained (Newsroom, 30 Jul 2024) that these features rest on three components, speech recognition, machine translation and text-to-speech, each trained on region-specific data with local institutions. Hebrew now has an unusually strong open base: Hebrew Whisper models (Apache-2.0) and speech data (CC-BY restricted, commercial training permitted) from ivrit.ai; Hebrew text models (Apache-2.0) from DICTA; commercial Hebrew speech and TTS already offered by Microsoft. This is ecosystem feasibility, not a Samsung implementation proposal. Samsung R&D Institute Israel (Herzliya) evaluates Israeli technologies for Samsung products.

## 5. The request

1. Add Hebrew to the next Galaxy AI language expansion on otherwise eligible devices.
2. If rollout must be phased: open an opt-in Hebrew beta with one communication feature (Live Translate or Interpreter) and one productivity feature (Transcript Assist or Note Assist).
3. Within 30 days: a named liaison, the current roadmap status, and the date of the next update.

## 6. The offer

A ready beta-tester group (7 of 10 respondents gave separate consent to beta contact), across models and use cases; introductions to Hebrew speech and language experts; an evaluation plan for conversational Hebrew. Unpaid, under written terms.

## 7. Principles

Factual, pro-product, no hostility. Every claim here rests on a dated primary source and appears in a public claims ledger with a corrections log. No media approach before Samsung has had the opportunity to respond to this dossier.
