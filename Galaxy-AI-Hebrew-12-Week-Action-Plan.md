# Galaxy AI in Hebrew: 12-Week Action Plan

Version: 1.0  
Prepared: 8 September 2026  
Campaign working name: Galaxy AI בעברית  
Campaign reference: HE-GALAXY-AI-2026

## 1. The outcome we are pursuing

The product goal is to add Hebrew to the Galaxy AI communication and productivity features that still exclude it on otherwise eligible devices:

- Call Assist, including Live Translate
- Interpreter
- Note Assist
- Transcript Assist

If Samsung needs a phased rollout, ask for an opt-in Hebrew beta containing at least:

- one communication feature: Live Translate or Interpreter; and
- one productivity feature: Transcript Assist or Note Assist.

The controllable 12-week campaign goal is:

> Obtain a named Samsung product or research liaison, a written roadmap status, and a dated next review milestone for Hebrew, backed by documented owner demand, qualified technical partners, and a beta-ready tester cohort.

Shipping the feature within 12 weeks is not a realistic campaign promise. Securing an internal review with a named owner is.

### The formal request to Samsung

> Add Hebrew to the next Galaxy AI language expansion on otherwise eligible devices, covering the currently unsupported communication and productivity features. If rollout must be phased, open an opt-in beta with at least one communication feature and one productivity feature. Within 30 calendar days, please provide a named liaison, the current roadmap status, and the date of the next decision or progress update.

In return, the campaign will offer:

- a documented cohort of Hebrew-speaking Galaxy owners;
- opt-in beta testers across devices and use cases;
- introductions to Hebrew speech, language, translation, and accessibility experts; and
- a rights-aware evaluation plan for real conversational Hebrew.

## 2. Important corrections to the starting proposal

These corrections should govern all public campaign material.

1. Hebrew is not completely absent from Galaxy AI. Samsung’s July 2026 matrix lists Hebrew for Writing Assist, Photo Assist, and Creative Studio, but not for Live Translate, Interpreter, Note Assist, or Transcript Assist. The campaign must describe that exact gap.  
   Source: [Samsung Israel Galaxy AI language matrix](https://www.samsung.com/il/galaxy-ai/)

2. Samsung Electronics Israel Ltd is a real Samsung-owned local entity. It is the first institutional escalation target. Suny is the official mobile importer and distributor, but it is an independent commercial partner and does not control Galaxy AI software.  
   Sources: [Samsung Israel service terms](https://www.samsung.com/il/support/service-terms-and-conditions/), [Samsung investor disclosure mentioning SEIL](https://www.samsung.com/sec/ir/reports-disclosures/public-disclosure/3740/), [Suny company description](https://www.samsungmobile.co.il/about)

3. Samsung Research Israel is a technical and open-innovation bridge, not a publicly confirmed owner of the Galaxy AI language roadmap. It should receive a technical partner proposal, not a consumer petition.  
   Source: [Samsung Research & Innovation Israel](https://research.samsung.com/sril)

4. Existing Hebrew technology shows feasibility, not a turnkey Samsung implementation. Samsung says Live Translate depends on separate ASR, neural machine translation, and TTS components, with local data and extensive validation.  
   Source: [Samsung’s description of Galaxy AI language development](https://news.samsung.com/global/recap-the-learning-curve-how-samsungs-rd-institutes-around-the-world-worked-on-galaxy-ai)

5. The licenses are mixed:

   - ivrit.ai repository and training code: MIT in relevant repositories;
   - ivrit.ai Whisper model: Apache 2.0;
   - ivrit.ai datasets: a modified/custom data license, not MIT;
   - DictaLM 3.0: Apache 2.0 open-weight text models;
   - Microsoft Hebrew speech and Hila/Avri voices: proprietary commercial cloud services.

   Sources: [ivrit.ai code](https://github.com/ivrit-ai/ivrit.ai), [ivrit.ai model](https://huggingface.co/ivrit-ai/whisper-large-v3), [ivrit.ai data license](https://www.ivrit.ai/en/the-license/), [DictaLM 3.0](https://dicta.org.il/dicta-lm-3), [Microsoft Speech language support](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/language-support)

6. Do not organize identical submissions. Use one campaign reference and one shared product request, but require every participant to describe their own device and use case. This produces stronger evidence and is less likely to be treated as duplicate traffic.

## 3. Default campaign choices

Unless the steering group decides otherwise:

- Audience: Hebrew speakers worldwide, with Israel reported as a separate segment.
- Scope: all four unsupported core features, while accepting a phased beta.
- Tone: factual, pro-product, firm, and nonpartisan.
- Public pressure: earned media only after private escalation and a 30-day response window.
- Budget: start Lean and release more spending only after the Week 6 demand gate.
- Data policy: minimal collection, adults only, separate consent for beta contact and publication.

If there are only three people:

- Person A: campaign lead and partnerships
- Person B: evidence, data, and privacy
- Person C: community, content, and media

Operating rules:

- Hold one 30-minute metrics and decisions meeting each week.
- Use one spokesperson in Hebrew and one in English; one person may cover both.
- Require two-person approval for every public factual claim.
- Maintain a shared decision log and a dated corrections log.
- Give only the data lead and one backup access to identifiable supporter data.
- Never imply that the campaign represents Samsung or any partner.
- Do not publish a partner’s name, logo, quote, or offer without written permission.

### Escalation sequence

1. Collect individualized Samsung Members or support submissions and case references.
2. Send a weekly aggregate to Samsung Electronics Israel through the ordinary contact route.
3. Once there is a meaningful base, send the complete dossier and request a meeting.
4. If there is no meaningful acknowledgment within 10 business days, use the local CEO-office form and reference the earlier contacts.
5. In parallel, send Suny a commercial request and SRIL a separate technical proposal.
6. Allow 30 calendar days from dossier delivery for a substantive response.
7. If the response is still absent or generic, publish the evidence report and begin targeted media outreach.
8. If Samsung supplies a named owner, a date, and a concrete next step, pause public escalation for 14 days and collaborate. Resume if the milestone is missed.

## 6. Response ladder

Use this to prevent a generic reply from being reported as a win.

| Level | Response |
|---|---|
| R0 | No response |
| R1 | Generic “forwarded to the relevant team” response |
| R2 | Confirmed internal case and a promised follow-up date |
| R3 | Named product/research contact and scheduled review |
| R4 | Written feasibility review, beta discussion, or roadmap milestone |
| R5 | Public commitment to a Hebrew release |

The 12-week target is R3. R4 or R5 is a stretch outcome and the longer-term product goal.

## 9. Supporter form and submission design

### Report these categories separately

- Confirmed supporter: a unique confirmed email.
- Self-reported Galaxy owner: a respondent who states that they currently own a Galaxy device.
- Documented Samsung submission: a unique case or reference number.
- Beta candidate: a person who separately opted into beta contact.

Never call every petition signer a verified owner.

### Minimum supporter survey fields

- Country or region
- Current Galaxy model
- One UI version, if known
- Primary languages
- Missing feature they would actually use
- Priority language pair
- Frequency and one concrete use case
- Current workaround and its friction or cost
- Whether the omission affected a purchase, upgrade, or retention decision
- Optional accessibility need
- Separate beta opt-in
- Separate permission to quote the story publicly
- An “I would not use these features” option to reduce leading bias

Do not collect an IMEI, serial number, receipt, identity document, Samsung password, address, or unredacted device screenshot.

### Individual Samsung request structure

Use a common subject:

> HE-GALAXY-AI-2026 - Hebrew for core Galaxy AI - [device model]

Every participant then answers in their own words:

1. Which Galaxy device and One UI version do you use?
2. Which unavailable Hebrew feature matters most?
3. Describe one real situation in which it would help.
4. What workaround do you use now?
5. Would you join an official opt-in beta?
6. Please record this as a product feature request and provide a reference number.

Do not provide a fully copy-paste body. One person should submit once and follow up by referencing the same case, not open new cases every week.

## 10. Evidence and demonstration protocol

Use an eligible Galaxy device and record:

- device model, region, One UI version, relevant app version, and recording date;
- Hebrew present in Writing Assist;
- Hebrew absent from Interpreter and Live Translate language selection;
- the current Hebrew behavior in Transcript Assist and Note Assist;
- a supported language working as the control.

Before recording:

- hide notifications, contacts, accounts, and personal notes;
- use scripted, non-sensitive content;
- preserve the uncut recordings;
- display mistakes as well as successes;
- test on more than one model if possible.

An optional second segment may run the same consented sample through ivrit.ai ASR and Microsoft Hebrew speech/TTS. Label it:

> Ecosystem feasibility example; not a Samsung implementation or a comparable product benchmark.

Useful stress cases include Hebrew-English code-switching, names, dates, numbers, noisy audio, unvocalized words, grammatical gender, and right-to-left/bidirectional display.

## 12. Beta-readiness and evaluation plan

The campaign should offer a recruitable cohort and evaluation design, not build an ungoverned voice corpus.

### Recruit for coverage

- eligible device generations and chipsets
- Israel and Hebrew speakers abroad
- age, gender, geography, and multilingual backgrounds
- Hebrew-English, Hebrew-Arabic, and Hebrew-Russian code-switching
- hearing, speech, vision, and motor accessibility needs
- quiet, noisy, phone-call, meeting, and far-field contexts

Start with adults. Keep beta-contact details separate from survey answers and link them using a random participant ID.

### Evaluation layers

| Layer | Hebrew cases | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| ASR | Phone codec, background noise, interruptions, code-switching, names, addresses, dates, money, acronyms | Word/character error, entity and number error |
| Translation | Gender agreement, negation, idioms, register, ambiguity, names and transliteration | Human adequacy, meaning preservation, severe-error rate |
| TTS | Unvocalized homographs, niqqud, acronyms, names, dates, currency, questions | Intelligibility, pronunciation, naturalness |
| Note and Transcript Assist | Meetings, lectures, multiple speakers, mixed languages, RTL bullets | Factual consistency, entity/number preservation, hallucinations, formatting |
| Interpreter and Live Translate | Turn-taking, overlap, noise, short corrections, connectivity changes | Task success, latency, meaning inversions, confidence |
| UX and accessibility | RTL/bidirectional text, large text, TalkBack, captions, Buds/hearing aids | Task completion and accessibility findings |

Define severe errors before testing: reversed negation, wrong number, wrong name, dangerous omission, or invented content. Do not announce product accuracy targets on Samsung’s behalf.

### Issue severity

- P0: privacy/security issue or dangerous meaning inversion
- P1: feature-blocking or systematically wrong result
- P2: material quality or usability problem
- P3: localization or polish problem

If Samsung engages, Samsung should distribute builds, define official logging and consent, and control beta security. The community should not sideload, reverse-engineer, or circulate proprietary packages.

## 13. Privacy and safety controls

- Publish a plain-language Hebrew/English privacy notice before collecting data.
- Explain purpose, fields, access, storage, retention, sharing, withdrawal, and a contact route.
- Separate petition signatures, survey answers, beta contacts, stories, and any recordings.
- Require separate consent to contact, quote, appear in media, participate in a beta, or contribute recordings.
- Enable two-factor authentication and restrict identifiable data to two people.
- Publish only aggregate results and suppress very small demographic groups.
- Set a deletion date and remove contact data when it is no longer needed.
- Do not transfer raw audio or personal data to Samsung or partners without written terms.
- Beta consent is not permission to train models.
- Obtain Israeli privacy/legal review before creating any reusable voice dataset.
- Never record a real private, customer-service, medical, or emergency call.
